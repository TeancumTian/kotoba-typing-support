打日语单词 Kotoba Typing

iOS App 支持页 · Support Page

「打日语单词」把背日语单词做成打字游戏：听发音，用手机上的日文键盘把单词打出来。单词按 JLPT N5–N1 分级、按主题分组，每组词打五轮，每轮拿走一个提示，打到不看也会。

Kotoba Typing turns Japanese vocabulary into a typing game: hear a word, then type it on a real Japanese keyboard. Words are graded JLPT N5–N1 and grouped by topic. Each set takes five rounds, and every round removes one hint until you can type the word from memory.

常见问题 · FAQ

什么是フリック？ 日本人在手机上的打法：点「か」出か，按住向左、上、右、下滑出き、く、け、こ。键上一直印着四周的小字，不用背键位。也可以在 设置 → 键盘 里换成罗马字（打 ka 出か）。

What is flick input? It is how people in Japan type on phones: tap か for か, or slide left, up, right or down for き, く, け, こ. The side characters are always printed on the keys. You can switch to a romaji keyboard (type "ka" for か) in Settings → Keyboard.

学习进度存在哪里？ 只存在你的设备上。删除 App 会一并清除。

Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

收费吗？ 完全免费，没有内购，没有广告。

Does it cost anything? No. It is free, with no in-app purchases and no ads.
